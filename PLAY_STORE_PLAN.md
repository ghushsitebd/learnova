# Learnova — Google Play Store Plan

## Store positioning
Learnova is a simple, child-friendly educational adventure game built around touch interaction, discovery and learning.

## Suggested title
Learnova — Kids Learning Adventure

## Short description
Learn letters, Arabic, Quran basics, animals and nature through a simple touch adventure.

## Full description
Learnova turns early learning into a gentle adventure.

Children can explore colorful worlds, vehicles, animals, nature and discovery activities with very simple touch controls. The learning path can introduce English letters first, followed by Arabic letters and Quran learning content.

### Designed for children
- Simple tap-to-play controls
- Clear visual learning cards
- Calm, easy-to-understand progression
- Child-friendly educational themes
- Nature, animals, vehicles and discovery worlds
- English and Arabic learning
- Quran learning content presented respectfully
- Lightweight offline-first gameplay

### Smart future-ready design
Learnova is structured so new worlds, vehicles, lessons and learning activities can be added without rebuilding the core game concept.

## Advertising approach
If advertising is enabled for the Play Store version:
- Ads must be child/family appropriate.
- Personalized advertising is disabled for child-directed users.
- Ads must not interrupt core gameplay.
- Ads are limited by an in-app frequency gate.
- The current product plan limits ads to at most 2 displays in a rolling 24-hour period.
- Ad placement should be outside active learning interactions.
- The production ad SDK and app ID will be connected only after the developer account/ad account is ready.

## Play Console preparation
Before publishing, verify:
1. Target audience and age groups.
2. Families/ads declarations.
3. Privacy policy URL.
4. App content questionnaire.
5. Data safety form.
6. Store listing screenshots and icon.
7. Content rating.
8. Production signing key.

## Important
Because Learnova is intended for children, the final advertising implementation must use a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK/source where required and must follow Google's current Families policy.
